# AIgorithmic Butterfly Effect: the sound of the calls

This branch holds only sound: small copies of the speech of the 2021 calls of
**[AIgorithmic Butterfly Effect](https://ranaji10.github.io/aigorithmic-butterfly-effect/)**, so the site can play each
hour under the words a small speech model wrote down from it in 2026.

`rNNN/KKK.webm` is ten minutes of recording `rNNN`, starting at second `KKK × 600` (mono Opus, 16 kHz, about 14 kbit/s,
cut from the originals without anything added). Which hours exist is listed per recording in `r/index.json` on the
`main` branch, field `aud`.

Before you listen: this is a person thinking aloud through weeks that ended badly for him, and friends who agreed to be
heard. Do not identify, profile or diagnose anyone from it. Anything here is removed on request: see the
[charter](https://github.com/ranaji10/aigorithmic-butterfly-effect/blob/main/CHARTER.md), section 6.
